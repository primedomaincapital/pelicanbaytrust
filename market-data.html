PelicanBayTrust.com Market Data

Pelican Bay sells at roughly twice the Naples county median, and it does so with a buyer base that pays cash and stays put. The trust question follows from that: wealth this concentrated needs fiduciaries, not just brokers.

Pelican Bay is a master-planned community inside Naples with its own arithmetic. Recent MLS-based reporting put its median sold price over the last 120 days at $1,297,500, against a Naples-area overall median closed price of $594,500 for full-year 2025 — a multiple of about 2.2 times, computed from those two published figures. A separate community guide put Pelican Bay's median listing price at $1.6 million for 2024–2025, with a recent median sale price of $1.2 million after an 8.7% pullback from prior peaks.

The community's scale explains part of the premium: 87 residential communities and subdivisions, three miles of beachfront, and two private beach clubs, according to the same community guide. Sellers in the recent 120-day window received 94.8% of asking price on average, with 112 homes for sale and 15 under contract — a market that is liquid at the top end without being hurried.

The county context matters for anyone thinking about trust services rather than transactions. Naples-area cash transactions ran near 58% of sales in the full-year 2025 data, and sales of homes over $5 million rose 16.6% in 2025. Cash buyers with multi-million-dollar holdings are the natural client base for trust administration, estate settlement, and family governance — the services a Pelican Bay trust practice would actually sell.

Reading the numbers

Three different Pelican Bay medians appear on this page — $1,297,500, $1.2 million, and $1.5 million — because they measure different things over different windows: recent MLS sales, a guide's recent-months figure, and a neighborhood survey. They are not errors to reconcile; they are evidence that a community with villas at one end and waterfront estates at the other has no single price.

No Pelican Bay-specific trust figure is shown. Trust administration for Pelican Bay residents happens through firms whose published AUM is regional, not community-level, and attributing a share of it to one community would be guesswork.

Methodology

Included: MLS-based Pelican Bay medians and market activity as published by a local brokerage citing FGCMLS via Stellar MLS, community-guide figures for scale and amenities, and Naples Area Board of REALTORS county figures for context. Excluded: individual property listings, any community-level trust or wealth total (none verifiable), and realtor-association figures that could not be traced to a public page in this session.
